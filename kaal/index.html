Kaal — Focus & Task Planner

An execution OS for high performers. Plan your day across the Covey four quadrants, build habits, capture notes, and keep your calendar in one calm, private place — on Android, iOS and Windows.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What Kaal does

  • Focus — a 2×2 priority matrix that keeps you on what matters.
  • Tasks & habits — lightweight planning, streaks and reviews.
  • Calendar — see your schedule and, optionally, connect Google Calendar (read-only) to view events alongside your plan.
  • Notes & inbox — capture quickly and turn items into tasks.

Your data stays yours

Kaal works fully offline on your device. If you sign in, your data syncs to a secured cloud account so it’s available across your devices. We never sell your data and never use your tasks, notes or connected-account data for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Google account access. Connecting Google Calendar uses a read-only scope so Kaal can display your events — Kaal never modifies or deletes them. Kaal’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
